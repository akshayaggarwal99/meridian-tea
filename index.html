Good tea, weighed out by someone who drinks it.

We're a loose-leaf tea shop and tea bar on N 45th St in Wallingford. About forty teas on the wall, a counter where you can taste before you buy, and a back room for tastings.

The shop

Teas by the ounce or the pound, blended and packed in the back. Handmade teaware from small potteries.

The tea bar

Hot or iced, by the cup. Our iced genmaicha outsold everything else this summer, by a lot.

Tea Club

Three teas a month, picked by us, with notes on how to brew them. How it works.

For cafés and offices

We pour in cafés, restaurants and a hotel kitchen around Seattle. Wholesale.

What we're drinking this month

Harbor Blend

Our house black blend with bitter orange peel. It keeps selling out at markets, so come early.

Autumn Sky oolong

A roasted oolong that tastes like the first cold morning of the year. On the wall now.

Genmaicha

Green tea with toasted rice. Nutty, cheap to drink every day, and very good iced.

Who we are

Nadia Osei opened Meridian after years of buying tea for other people's shops. Today it's a crew of twelve: Priya runs the floor, Marcus packs every online order, and Jess looks after the Tea Club and our wholesale cafés. We buy from importers and growers we know by name, and we'd rather tell you a tea is out than sell you something stale.
